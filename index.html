What Is Street Fighter (2026) About?

The story is set in 1993 and follows two fighters, Ryu and Ken Masters, who were once close but have become estranged. Their lives take a new turn when the mysterious Chun-Li recruits them for the next World Warrior Tournament.

The tournament is more than a simple contest of fighting skills. Behind the battles lies a dangerous conspiracy that forces the competitors to confront their pasts and question their loyalties. Ryu and Ken must navigate their complicated relationship while facing powerful opponents and unexpected threats.

The premise brings together the tournament structure familiar to fans of the games with a cinematic story about rivalry, fate and personal conflict. Each fighter enters the competition with a distinct fighting style, personality and history.

As the competition develops, the characters are pushed into increasingly dangerous confrontations. Their choices matter beyond the ring, and the conspiracy behind the tournament threatens to turn the entire event into something far more serious than a championship.

Street Fighter Movie Cast and Characters

Noah Centineo as Ken Masters

Noah Centineo plays Ken Masters, a skilled fighter whose history with Ryu is central to the movie. Ken is known in the franchise for his aggressive combat style, confidence and rivalry with his longtime friend.

Andrew Koji as Ryu

Andrew Koji portrays Ryu, one of the franchise's most iconic characters. Ryu is a dedicated martial artist whose journey focuses on discipline, self-improvement and the pursuit of becoming a stronger fighter.

Callina Liang as Chun-Li

Callina Liang plays Chun-Li, the mysterious fighter who brings Ryu and Ken into the World Warrior Tournament. Chun-Li is one of the most recognizable characters in the Street Fighter universe.

Roman Reigns as Akuma

WWE superstar Roman Reigns, whose real name is Joe Anoa'i, portrays Akuma. The character is associated with formidable fighting ability and an intimidating presence. His casting is particularly exciting for wrestling fans who want to see Reigns in a major video game adaptation.

Cody Rhodes as Guile

WWE star Cody Rhodes plays Guile, a military fighter recognized for his distinctive appearance and powerful combat techniques. His involvement gives wrestling audiences another familiar face to look forward to.

Jason Momoa as Blanka

Jason Momoa plays Blanka, the distinctive fighter known for his unusual appearance and powerful attacks. Momoa's action-movie background makes him a notable member of the ensemble.

Other Notable Cast Members

The cast also includes David Dastmalchian as M. Bison, Curtis “50 Cent” Jackson as Balrog, Vidyut Jammwal as Dhalsim, Andrew Schulz as Dan Hibiki, Eric André as Don Sauvage, Orville Peck as Vega and Olivier Richters as Zangief.

What Makes Street Fighter Different From Other Action Movies?

Street Fighter combines martial arts action with a roster of distinctive fighters whose techniques and personalities are familiar to longtime fans. Instead of following just one hero, the story brings several rivals and competitors into the same tournament.

The rivalry between Ryu and Ken gives the movie an emotional centre, while Chun-Li's involvement brings the two fighters back into a dangerous competition. The conspiracy behind the tournament adds another layer beyond the matches themselves.

The adaptation also has an unusual mix of performers from film, comedy, professional wrestling and martial arts. That variety helps distinguish the movie from a conventional action film and makes it appealing to several different audiences.
